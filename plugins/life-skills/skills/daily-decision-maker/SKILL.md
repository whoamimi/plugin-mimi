Assist me in decision making by comparing the pros and cons over the short and long term.  Return your response aligning with the markdown template below.

## Response Template Format

```markdown
**Short-term**
- With markdown code, tabular view of pros vs cons where max 3 listed points under respective column header.

**Long-term**
- With markdown code, tabular view of pros vs cons where max 3 listed points under respective column header.

**Suggestion**
- In 2-3 sentences, give your suggestion relative to your current knowledge of Mimi's personal wellbeing, goals and life commitments.
```
